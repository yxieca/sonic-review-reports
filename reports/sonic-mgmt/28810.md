## Summary
The collection hook previously returned from the entire item loop when an ASIC-marked item already contained `check_dut_asic_type`. Parametrized siblings share their function's fixture-name list: inserting the guard into the first sibling also guards the other siblings. Encountering the second sibling therefore stopped the hook, leaving later **functions and modules** without their ASIC guard.

Changing `return` to `continue` at `tests/common/plugins/custom_fixtures/__init__.py:10–12` correctly preserves the existing guard and continues processing later items. The insertion path at13–18, actual guard, skip assertion, and plugin registration are unchanged. The title, description, linked issue and implementation agree.

Concrete source path: root `tests/conftest.py:137–158` registers the plugin; ECMP tests at205–309 provide two five-case parametrized functions; Cisco-specific VXLAN/ACL modules supply later marked callers. `check_dut_asic_type.py:5–15` compares the current node's nearest ASIC mark with the selected frontend DUT and invokes the existing `pytest_require` skip exception. Their capability declarations—not incidental failures—justify enforcing this guard.
