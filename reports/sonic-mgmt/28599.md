## Summary

The four edits in `tests/bgp/test_bgp_dual_asn.py:176–177,184–185` exclude both inclusive endpoints from each IPv4 speaker's random range. The two ranges are child subnets generated from the selected primary VLAN. Excluding each child's boundaries is coherent: PTF interface configuration uses that child's prefix, not the larger parent VLAN prefix.

Full source and consumers show the selected addresses feed PTF interface setup, ExaBGP local-address/router-id, neighbor establishment checks, route next hops and flap counters. The two subnet objects and their ASN/listen-range associations are unchanged. Both positive and negative dual-ASN assertions remain intact; no skips, test suppression, fixture or teardown changes were introduced.

## Non-blocking nits and scope limits

No actionable in-scope nits retained. Two limitations are worth keeping distinct from this fix:

- **Address-in-use collisions remain pre-existing.** The DUT VLAN address can still be selected. Coordinator controls reproduce `192.168.0.1` on both base and head. This patch fixes network/broadcast endpoints, not occupied-address allocation.
- **Very small custom VLANs are not qualified.** A parent /30 or /31 becomes child /31 or /32 ranges, and the new bounds produce an empty `randint` range. This was executed and confirmed, not assumed. The staged t0/t0-64/dualtor VLAN inputs use /21–/24 parents; no supported affected small-VLAN deployment was established. Retain as a portability limit rather than manufacture a blocker or require a generalization unrelated to current inputs.

IPv6 selection and existing cleanup/assertion quirks are unchanged; they are not new findings against this PR.
