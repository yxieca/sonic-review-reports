## Blocking issue

### P2 — Establish equal per-rank entitlement before asserting raw cross-queue fairness

**Anchors:** [`test_rocev2_qp_fairness_dcqcn.py:81–107`](https://github.com/sonic-net/sonic-mgmt/blob/90125a5622a28de95d79c22d240fa1b887d33a8d/tests/snappi_tests/rocev2/test_rocev2_qp_fairness_dcqcn.py#L81-L107), lines128–141 and195–200. Reached `files/helper.py:813–855` (`build_incast_topology`),960–981 (`build_rank_fairness`).

The test permits any number of ports≥4, uses every configured port except one receiver, and cycles senders betweenQ3 andQ4. It then compares raw achieved rates across all senders against one30% spread bound. Although scheduler configuration is collected, neither the queue weights nor their per-rank entitlement enter a guard or expectation.

With the PR's stated weights20:10:

| Accepted geometry | Sender split Q3/Q4 | Correct weighted per-rank shares of100 units | Raw spread |
|---|---|---|---|
|4 ports /3 senders|2/1|33.33,33.33,33.33|0%|
|5 ports /4 senders|2/2|33.33,16.67,33.33,16.67|50%|

Thus equal sharing **within each queue** and correct DWRR scheduling do not imply the raw cross-queue equality asserted by the new test. Port fixtures return all discovered ports; no hidden exact-four-port guard was found. Even selecting exactly four ports alone is insufficient unless the2:1 queue-weight premise is also checked.

**Recommended change:** either arrange/select and verify a topology/QoS configuration with equal per-rank entitlement, skipping incompatible configurations clearly, or check within-queue rank fairness and normalize cross-queue expectations by scheduler weights and sender counts. Do not simply increase tolerance.

**Evidence:** Coordinator executed AST-extracted pinned `build_incast_topology`, `dwrr_expected_rates` and exact scalar deviation expressions with positive/negative controls. Four-port and five-port results are retained in `validation.txt`/`topology-validation.json`. This proves the prerequisite mismatch, not a live hardware failure or full pandas integration run.
