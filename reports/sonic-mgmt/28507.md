## Summary

The change retains MAC learning and accounting across all eligible VLAN members, while limiting **BSL forwarding selection to 20,000 distinct unordered pairs per VLAN and packet-type instance**. It generates a bounded near/far ring prefix directly rather than generating every combination and slicing afterward. Non-BSL retains the original pair identities, direction and order.

The BSL pair set is exhaustive through 200 members (19,900 pairs); sampling starts at 201. For 512 members it selects 20,000 of 130,816 possible pairs. Every member remains represented at realistic reviewed scales, and selected endpoints retain the correct port groups and tag modes. BSL direction/order intentionally differ from the base; neither implementation tests both directions of every pair.
